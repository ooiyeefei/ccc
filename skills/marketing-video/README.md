# marketing-video

Product and solution marketing films for **strangers**: a pain in their own words, a value with one real proof moment, one limit said once, one ask. High level, non-technical, never a feature tour. Also the long "a month in the life of a [industry] business" flagship.

Leading words: **stranger**, **pain**, **proof moment**.

## Use this, not that

- A film that ends on "try it" for someone who has never heard of the product -> here.
- A pitch to judges, investors or a committee in a slot -> `pitch-package`. A how-to for a user finishing a task -> `help-center-tutorial`.
- Staging and QA -> `film-line`. Recording -> `demo-video`. Rendered scenes and platform cuts -> `motion-video`.

## Structure

- `SKILL.md` - the brief, the blocking storyboard, the route (filmed or rendered), the checks
- `references/story.md` - length ladder, spine, finding the pain, value to proof moment, endings
- `references/industry-flagship.md` - the industry brief and the month-in-the-life shape; cutting shorter films and tutorials from the same takes
