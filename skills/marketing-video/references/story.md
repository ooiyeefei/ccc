# Story - the length ladder, the spine, the pain

## The length ladder

Pick the rung before the storyboard. Each rung is its own storyboard drawn from the same take bank; a shorter film is never a trimmed longer one, because the proof moments that survive a cut are different ones.

| Rung | Length | Carries | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut-down | 15 to 30 s | one pain, one proof moment, the ask | captions, voice optional |
| Single value | 30 to 45 s | one pain, one value | voice or captions |
| Per workflow | 60 to 90 s | pain, turn, two or three values, limit, ask | voice, captions burned in |
| Overview | 2 to 3 min | a day of one role, every value once | voice |
| Flagship | 8 to 12 min | a month of one business, in chapters | voice, chapters |

Say the length honestly. A film that cannot reach a requested length without padding is reported as short, with the levers that would lengthen it, never padded.

## The spine

**Pain, turn, values, limit, ask.**

| Beat | What it does | Share of a 75 s film |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | The stranger recognises their week. Spoken first, with no logo. The frame is a prop, a plain card or a product state that shows the pain | 0:00 to 0:08 |
| Turn | States the promise in one sentence; the product appears here, mid-action | 0:08 to 0:16 |
| Values | Two or three, each one proof moment of about 12 seconds | 0:16 to 1:00 |
| Limit | The one honest edge, said once, plainly, over the screen where the edge sits | 1:00 to 1:08 |
| Ask | One thing, in its exact words, last heard | 1:08 to 1:15 |

Twelve seconds is a starting size for a value, not a law: two values at 18 seconds or three at 14 both fit. Scale the spine to the rung; the shares hold, the seconds move.

A value beat has one shape: the outcome in the stranger's words, then the proof moment, then what it means for them. Order values by the stranger's cost, biggest pain first.

## Finding the pain

Take it from what strangers said, never from what the product does: support threads, sales calls, reviews, the owner's own words. Write it as a moment with a time and an object. Test it by reading it aloud to someone from the trade who has never heard of us; if they have to ask what a word means, the word goes. A pain written in our category word ("reconciliation backlog") is a feature in disguise.

## Value to proof moment

State each value as an outcome the viewer feels, then name the real frame that shows it.

| Value (the stranger's words) | Proof moment |
|---|---|
| "I stop retyping receipts" | a receipt dropped in and the form filling itself |
| "I can see where a figure came from" | a click on a total and the bill behind it opening |
| "My accountant gets everything" | the export finishing and listing what it made |

A proof moment needs product motion the viewer sees: a cursor travelling, text landing, a list changing. A still with a camera move is not one.

No length given: propose the per-workflow rung, say so, and let the owner move it.

## Endings

One ask, and the last line heard: try it, talk to us, watch the next film. The close can land the promise in new words first, never a verbatim repeat. No price or plan name unless the brief names it. No thanks card.

## Honesty

Claim only what a frame shows. The limit is said once, as what the product does and where its edge is. A rendered figure is marked illustrative on screen. Figures reconcile: a mocked ledger still balances.

## Formats

A feed film is watched muted: burned captions, the proof readable without sound, nothing critical inside a platform's caption or button zone. Per-platform ratios and safe zones: `motion-video/references/platform-formats.md`.
