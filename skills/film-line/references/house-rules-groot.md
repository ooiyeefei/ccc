# House rules - Groot Finance (swap this file for another company's)

The only company-specific file in the film skills. It holds wording and constraints, never ids or credentials. For Groot films it wins over `demo-video/references/distribution.md` and `structure.md` where they differ: the "demonstration purposes" cards, the "Demo purposes only" label, the "Not for redistribution" line and the example bank name "Bank Contoh" (contoh means sample) are replaced by the wording below. Everything else in those files, including the questions to settle at the gate, stands.

## Words and data

- Banned on screen, in typed data, in file names and on cards: demo, test, sample, UAT, probe, presenter, designated.
- No real brand or bank in any frame: banks, marketplaces, fuel and payment brands, accounting competitors.
- E-mail addresses end `@film.hellogroot.com`. The staff domain `@hellogroot.com` and any personal address never appear.
- Workspace names are plain made-up trading names, never "Demo".
- Money in ringgit, dates in the business's own zone. Record after 08:00 Malaysia time on the day, because some screens pre-fill the UTC date (08:00 Malaysia time is UTC midnight).
- Banned words in Malay and Indonesian too: contoh, ujian, percubaan, demonstrasi, sampel.

## Band, end card, voice

- Band under the picture: the Groot monogram at the left, burned captions in the middle, "(c) <year> Groot Finance" at the right.
- End card, 3 seconds, silent: the wordmark, `finance.hellogroot.com`, "Try it free", and "Names, documents and figures shown are fictitious." No price, no customer number, no word "demonstration".
- Voice Kokoro `af_heart` at speed 1.0, voiced after the take. Music: a CC0 bed ("Chill lofi inspired") dipped 8 dB under speech. Captions burned in, plus an `.srt`; chapters for YouTube. A 1.3x variant is the default player for the long flagship, with the 1.0x film beside it.

## Honest wording

- "Your books and your reports", never "your annual accounts" or "a full set" until year-end close, the equity statement and comparatives are all live.
- "The ledger" here is the product's own accounting ledger, not the write ledger of `staging.md`. For strangers the voice says "your books"; "the ledger" stays on screen where the product shows it and in chapters for the accountant. Worked out from the ledger. Checked against the books. Suggests, you accept. A person presses every post, approval and payment, on camera.
- Groot never pays anyone and never moves money. The transfer is made in the customer's bank.
- Sales e-invoice submission to LHDN is early access: say so in one sentence or leave it out. No bank-feed claim.
- Tax: facts and calculations only. No advice on reducing tax, and a disclaimer on every tax screen shown.
- Income tax deduction (PCB) is typed in, not calculated. EPF, SOCSO and EIS are worked out, not filed or paid.
- No traction figures, no customer counts, no price.
- A herd, stock or asset value is never implied. Cattle bought are recorded as a cost.

## Distribution

- Marketing films are public: the website embeds YouTube.
- Tutorials are for signed-in users only. Hosting is not decided. Unlisted YouTube is not private, so it is not an option for tutorials.
- A recording day, a deadline or an ETA is never stated by an agent unless the owner named it.
